Two-Way Pager · Fall 2026 · In Progress

Battery-powered pagers that text over the 915 MHz ISM band, on a 2-layer board targeting 1 km range at under $40 per board.

Hands-On PCB Engineering (EE 198) · 4-person team · Fall 2026

The goal

Our team is designing a 2-layer KiCad PCB for battery-powered pagers that text each other over the 915 MHz ISM band. Each board pairs an ESP32-S3 with a CC1101 transceiver and RF matching network, targeting 1 km range at under $40 per board.

What I'm doing

I co-designed the power and radio schematics, including USB-C LiPo charging with ESD and reverse current protection, a switched 3.3 V LDO rail, and the CC1101's 915 MHz matching network, and reviewed the remaining sheets.

Status

  • In progress: schematics and PCB design underway, not yet built
