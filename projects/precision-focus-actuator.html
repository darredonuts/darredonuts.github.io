Precision Focus Actuator · Fall 2025

As a team, we worked with Malin Space Science Systems through Engineering Solutions at Berkeley to design a focus actuator for space camera applications used primarily in NASA rovers. We evaluated several actuation approaches before settling on a mechanical design, then built and validated a working prototype.

My work on this project was the control systems and algorithms. I designed a bidirectional homing and midpoint referencing algorithm using limit switches and a Hall sensor to compute and compensate for mechanical backlash, achieving 0.139 mil actuation per degree of rotation and overall actuation precision of ±2.5 mil.
